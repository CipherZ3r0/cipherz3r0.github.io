## Some Code, Some Design, and a Change of Scenery

Not the most eventful week on the work front, but a few things kept me occupied. A bit of client work, writing Go for the first time, and messing around with some designs. Here's a quick look.

## A little client work

Spent some time handling things for one of my projects, mostly around client communication, figuring out what needed attention, and getting things moving.

It's a part of working on projects that doesn't always show up in screenshots or Git commits, but there's quite a bit of it. Not everything can be solved by opening VS Code, unfortunately.

## Trying out Go

I've been working on a monitoring tool using Go + react + postgres (one of the best combo, according to me atleast), and this is actually my first time working with the language.

Building it for our own internal use. The idea is to build something that can keep an eye on websites and databases, check their health, track metrics, and eventually help me monitor multiple applications from one place.

There's been a fair amount of learning along the way, especially getting used to Go's way of doing things. Coming from Python, some things feel familiar, while others have me rethinking how I usually write code(AI's been a big help in getting me up to speed with Go, though ✌️😅).

Still working on it, though...

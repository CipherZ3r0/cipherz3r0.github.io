## A Little About "ME"

Somewhere along the way, I developed a habit of not leaving things alone.

A database query takes longer than it should, and I want to know why. A library behaves unexpectedly, and I end up reading its source code. A system works, but I don't quite understand what's happening underneath, and that becomes a reason to investigate and that became the reason i ended up exploring multiple different domains from development to security, from marketing to business ending up handling clients many times for the things i build...

Heyaaa, I'm Nilax Modi, a computer engineering student and software developer, also known online as CipherZer0 and also some times NIX. I enjoy building software which people actually use and which make life even just a little easier, exploring unfamiliar technologies, and understanding the mechanisms behind the things I use.

My interests have gradually taken me across backend engineering, databases, Python, systems programming, cybersecurity, AI and retrieval systems, and open-source software. While these areas may seem different, I find myself approaching them with a similar curiosity: understanding how things work, identifying where they fall short, and exploring how they can be improved.

## Things I build and explore

I've worked on projects involving AI-powered retrieval, software security, business applications, and tooling. I've also contributed to open-source projects, which has given me the opportunity to work with established codebases, investigate real problems, and learn from the people maintaining software used by others.

Some of the things I find particularly interesting are:

- **Backend & databases:** Designing APIs, investigating database behaviour, concurrency, and improving performance.
- **Python & systems:** Exploring Python internals, native extensions, runtime compatibility, and gradually moving closer to lower-level programming.
- **AI & retrieval systems:** Experimenting with retrieval pipelines, ranking, context, and making AI systems more useful and reliable.
- **Security:** Understanding vulnerabilities, attack surfaces, and how systems can be designed to withstand unexpected behaviour.
- **Open source:** Contributing fixes, improvements, documentation, and experiments while learning how real software evolves.

These aren't boxes I intend to stay within. They're areas I'm actively exploring, and I'm always interested in problems that introduce me to something new.

## Learning through the work

Some of the most valuable things I've learned haven't come from building something entirely from scratch, but from investigating something that already exists.

Working through performance problems in large codebases, exploring concurrency behaviour, adapting software to newer runtimes, or understanding an unfamiliar implementation often requires more than simply making the code work. It requires patience, experimentation, reading, debugging, and sometimes reconsidering the assumptions you started with.

That's the kind of work I enjoy only thanks to curiosity :)

I don't expect to know everything before starting a project. I'd rather approach a problem with a solid foundation, investigate what I don't understand, and leave with a better understanding than I had before.

## Why I built this website

A lot of what I work on otherwise lives in repositories, terminal sessions, notes, experiments, pull requests, and the occasional debugging rabbit hole.

I wanted a place to bring some of that together.

This website is where I'll document projects, technical explorations, open-source contributions, ideas, and lessons that I think are worth sharing. Some writing will be about things I've built; other pieces will be about things I wanted to understand.

It's also a record of my progress as an engineer/builder/developer, not just the finished results.

I'm still learning, still building, and still finding new things to investigate.

If you're here because of a project, an open-source contribution, a referral, or simply curiosity, welcome. I'm always glad to connect with people who enjoy building things and figuring out how they work.

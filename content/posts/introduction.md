## A Little About "ME"

Heyaaa 👋, I'm Nilax Modi, a computer engineering student and software developer. I go by CipherZer0 online, and sometimes just NIX.

I've worked on quite a few different things over the past few years, from web development and backend systems to cybersecurity, AI, and even some marketing and business-related work through projects and client work. Basically, I've never been particularly good at sticking to just one thing. 😅

I wouldn't say I have a single area I'm focused on. I've been interested in quite a few things, and that interest keeps changing. Lately, I've been spending more time around backend engineering, databases, Python, systems programming, AI and retrieval systems, and open-source software.

## Things I work on

(It's scattered very much which you might not like it🙃) 

Most of my work has been through projects, experiments, client work and contributions to existing software. Some things I've built myself, while others involved working with codebases that were already being used by people.

A few things I've spent time working on:

- **Backend & databases**: Building APIs, working with databases, and dealing with performance and concurrency-related problems. Sometimes the database is the problem, sometimes it's the code, and sometimes it's me.

- **Python & systems**: Python development, exploring internals, runtime compatibility, and some lower-level programming. Python has a lot going on behind that deceptively simple syntax.

- **AI & retrieval systems**: Working with RAG, retrieval pipelines, embeddings, and experimenting with ways to improve these systems. Because apparently getting an LLM to answer a question correctly isn't always as simple as asking it nicely. Some other automations are also there which were build for some clients.

- **Security**: Learning about vulnerabilities, security research, and how applications can be made more secure. Breaking things, but with permission, of course. :)

- **Open source**: Contributing fixes, improvements, documentation, and working on issues in existing projects. Also, occasionally staring at code wondering where on earth to begin.

I've still got plenty to learn in all of these areas, and I'm sure I'll end up working on things that aren't even on this list yet. My browser tabs can probably confirm that.

## Learning through the work 

Working on projects doesn't always go as planned. Sometimes something breaks, sometimes the problem turns out to be somewhere completely different from where you thought it was, and sometimes you have to spend a while understanding code you didn't write before you can make any changes.

I've encountered plenty of that while working on projects and contributing to open source. It's not always straightforward. You try things, things don't work, you try something else, and somewhere in between you figure out what's going on. Or at least figure out why the previous thing didn't work. Progress is progress. :')

I still have a lot to learn, and most of it will probably happen while working on things rather than planning to learn them beforehand (cause for me planned things doesn't always turns out how i intended it).

## Why I built this website

Most of my work ends up on GitHub, in project repositories, or scattered across notes and documentation. And not everything there is particularly easy to follow without some context.

I'll use this website to write about projects, things I've been working on, technical topics I find interesting, open-source contributions, and whatever else I feel like sharing. Some posts might be technical, others might be about a project or something I came across or others will just be about something i am going to do other then work.

I also wanted a place outside of GitHub to have a little more context around the things I work on, without having to turn every update into a README.

So yeah, that's pretty much it. No grand plan for this website (yet). I'll keep adding things here as I go, and hopefully it'll be interesting to look back at a year or two from now.

If you've somehow ended up here, welcome! Have a look around, and feel free to contact me and have conversation on anything or just saying "Hi" will also work 😁 (these thing cost nothing so do reach out and let's connect).

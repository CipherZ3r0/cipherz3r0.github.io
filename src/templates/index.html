About

I rarely stop at making something work. I want to know why it works, where it breaks, and what happens when you push it further.

That curiosity has taken me from tracing database bottlenecks and exploring Python internals to building retrieval systems, experimenting with security, and contributing to software I use. I don't have a fixed lane yet, and I don't particularly want one. I'm here to explore the parts of computing that make me curious, build things along the way, and document what I discover.
